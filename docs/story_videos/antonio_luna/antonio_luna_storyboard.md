# Antonio Luna — Maikling Kuwento ng Isang Heneral — Storyboard

**Final sequence:** exactly six illustrated scenes; 88.5 seconds; 1280 × 720 / 24 fps.
**Camera animation:** slow pan/zoom of painted plates, plus short contained fades. This is an illustrated narrated short, not full character animation. No lip-sync or invented conversations.
**Subtitle typography:** existing OFL-licensed Roboto Bold, 34 px, white, 2.5 px dark outline and shadow; two lines maximum; 64 px side and 40 px bottom margins.

## Character continuity

One consistent historically inspired Antonio Luna, Filipino man in his early thirties, natural adult proportions, medium warm-brown complexion, broad oval face, deep-set dark-brown eyes, strong straight eyebrows, short neatly side-parted black hair, and a prominent neatly groomed dark mustache; no beard. Use the SAME face, mustache, hairline, build, and eye shape across every scene. Base likeness on archival Antonio Luna portrait references, never an actor or another hero. Skin, fabric colors and lighting are illustrative reconstructions, not claims about exact archival colors.

Civilian dress in scenes 1–2; the same period-inspired officer uniform in scenes 3–4 and 6. Exact colors and incidental background figures are illustrative, not separately documented biographical facts.

Scene 6's portrait was generated first using a public-domain archival portrait as a likeness guide. That generated identity was reused for the civilian and officer scenes. Scene 5 is an empty setting. Exact costume colors, backgrounds and anonymous officers are illustrative reconstructions, not claims about a particular meeting.
